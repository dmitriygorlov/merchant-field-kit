# Merchant Field Kit exploratory benchmark: UCI Online Retail II

Adapted from Chen, D. (2012), *Online Retail II*, UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D · CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Use the Jan–Nov 2011 full calendar months inside the second workbook sheet (exclude both boundary months) of the repository's official archive, SHA-256 572e36277c2390fbfde10664750731e0a86f55e33470d91919085f0408e67bfb. Read only Invoice, InvoiceDate, Quantity and Price. Exclude cancellation invoices (C prefix), nonpositive quantity and nonpositive price. Aggregate positive units by invoice date. No identifiers or raw lines are included here. A date absent from the file is explicitly interpreted as zero qualifying units recorded by this source, not certified zero demand, shop closure or proof of completeness. All Saturdays are absent, as are a few other dates; no date becomes absent only because of the filter. This is an invoice-based proxy for gross ordered units.

The resulting daily CSV has 334 dates and 4,914,889 units across 463,513 qualifying source lines, from one historical shop, with many wholesale customers. Run the MVP's run_forecast(load_csv(...)) on `docs/benchmark_daily.csv` without tuning. On the same five rolling 28-day windows, MAE was 6,937.46 (Prophet), 7,164.93 (repeat last week) and 10,686.01 (last four weeks average). Relative difference from the stronger baseline was 3.17%; Prophet won three of five overlapping windows, and the repeat-week baseline won the most recent window. No independent holdout or causal evidence of business benefit. Fold values are in benchmark_folds.csv.
